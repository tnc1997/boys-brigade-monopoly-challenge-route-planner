Setup

Locations

One per row, as given on the sheet, like a street or place in Bristol. Each is looked up when you move on to the next row. If one isn't found or is in the wrong spot, tap 📍 and then tap where it is on the map. Addresses are looked up with OpenStreetMap Nominatim, © OpenStreetMap contributors.

    Enter every location before planning, for the most accurate route. Locations added afterwards show as + on the map until you plan again.

    Route

    Uses your current location and time, and the locations still to visit.

    Add your locations above and press Plan route.

    Long-press the map to add a location there.

    Some map tiles couldn't load without signal. Your route is still shown, and the List tab works offline.
